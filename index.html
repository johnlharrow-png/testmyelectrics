How often does a rental property need an EICR?

In England, landlords must have the electrical installation inspected and tested at least every five years under the Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector Regulations 2020.

You need to give a copy of the report to your tenants within 28 days, and to the local council within 7 days if they ask for it. Councils can fine landlords up to £30,000 for breaches.

What's an EICR, and do homeowners need one?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a full inspection and test of your wiring, fuse board, sockets, lighting and earthing, checked against the UK wiring regulations (BS 7671).

For owner-occupied homes, an EICR is recommended at least every 10 years, and it's a smart move before you buy a property or start a renovation.

How much does it cost?

All prices exclude VAT. A domestic EICR covers up to 8 circuits and costs £79 for a studio, rising to £140 for a six-bedroom home, plus £15 for each extra circuit. Parking and congestion charges are added to the final bill if they apply. PAT testing is £59.95 for up to 10 items, then £1.50 per extra item. A smoke, heat and CO alarm check is £50 for up to 6 alarms.

Commercial fixed wire testing starts at £169 for up to 10 circuits, plus £10 per extra circuit, and emergency lighting tests start at £80. Larger sites are quoted, and remedial work is only ever done with your approval.

What happens if my property fails?

Any C1 (danger present), C2 (potentially dangerous) or FI (further investigation needed) code makes a report "unsatisfactory". C3 codes are recommendations and don't fail the report.

We'll explain what we found and send a clear quote for any remedial work. Landlords must complete remedial work within 28 days, or sooner if the report says so.

How long does a test take, and will the power be off?

Times can vary, but an EICR takes no longer than 2–3 hours. The power will be off for short periods while circuits are tested, so it's worth saving any work and resetting clocks afterwards.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes. You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Please make sure someone can let us in at the agreed time: if no one is on site, the full charge still applies. See our booking terms.

Where do I find my certificate?

It's emailed to you and saved in My account, along with your invoice and any quotes. We email you a login link, so there's no password to remember.

I have more than one fuse board or several properties. Can I book online?

Online prices cover one fuse board in one property. For homes with more boards, portfolios of rental properties, or business premises, request a callback. We'll call you with a price for everything and book all the visits for you.

Is PAT testing a legal requirement?

There's no law that names PAT testing specifically. But landlords and employers have a legal duty to keep electrical equipment safe, including under the Electricity at Work Regulations 1989. PAT testing is the standard way to show you've done that, and many insurers and letting agents expect it.

Can you test schools and restaurants out of hours?

Yes. We schedule school testing into holidays and after hours, and test restaurants before service or overnight, so nobody's day is disrupted. Request a business quote and we'll plan it with you.
