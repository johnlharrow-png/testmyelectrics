What changes the price

  1. The size of your home. Prices go up with the number of bedrooms, from a studio to six bedrooms.
  2. The number of circuits. Up to 8 are included. Each extra circuit is £15 + VAT, counted by our electrician on the day.
  3. More than one consumer unit. Some homes have a second board, for example for an annexe or a garage. We price these for you. If we find a second board when we arrive, we tell you before we test it whether there is any extra charge.
  4. Parking and congestion charges. Only added if we have to pay them to reach you, at cost.

Any extras go on a final bill in your online account. Your certificate is issued once it is paid. Several properties or more than one board? Email bookings@testmyelectrics.com for a price.

If your report is Unsatisfactory

  1. A report is Unsatisfactory if it has at least one C1 (danger present) or C2 (potentially dangerous). C3s are recommendations and don't fail it.
  2. For a C1, we make it safe before we leave, with your agreement, usually by switching off that circuit. You get a written danger notice the same day.
  3. We explain each item in plain English and send a fixed quote for the work. Nothing is done without your OK.
  4. Once the work is done, you get a certificate for it. Keep it with the report to show the problems have been put right.
  5. Landlords have 28 days to have the work done, or less if the report says so. The rules for landlords.

EICR cost questions

How much does an EICR cost?

Our domestic EICR is from £79 + VAT for a studio flat, and £140 + VAT for a six-bedroom home. Every price includes up to 8 circuits and one consumer unit. You see the full price for your property before you pay.

Are your prices with or without VAT?

Our website shows prices without VAT. VAT is added at 20%, and the booking page shows the full price, including VAT, before you pay.

How do I know how many circuits I have?

Each circuit has its own fuse or circuit breaker in your fuse board, so counting them gives you a rough idea. Most homes have 8 or fewer. You do not need to count them before you book: our electrician counts them on the day.

What if you find more circuits than I booked?

Each circuit over 8 is £15 + VAT. We tell you, and the extra goes on a final bill in your online account. Your certificate is issued once that bill is paid.

Is the cost of repairs included?

No. If something needs fixing, we explain it in plain English and send you a fixed quote for the work. Nothing is done without your OK. The exception is a C1 (danger present): with your agreement we make it safe before we leave, usually by switching off that circuit.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes, free of charge, up to 24 hours before your appointment, through your online account or by email.
