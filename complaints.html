Complaints procedure

Last updated: 16 November 2026

We want to put it right

We aim to get every test right first time. If something hasn't gone the way it should, please tell us. Most problems are sorted quickly once we know about them.

1. How to complain

Email us at bookings@testmyelectrics.com, or write to:

Complaints
TestMyElectrics (UK Sustainability Group Limited)
Unit 21, Industrial Estate, Old Church Road
East Hanningfield, Essex CM3 8AB

Please include:

  • your name and the best way to contact you;
  • the address of the property and your booking reference, if you have it;
  • what went wrong, and when;
  • what you'd like us to do to put it right.

Photos or copies of documents are helpful but not essential.

2. What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and tell you who's dealing with it.
  2. We look into it. We'll review your booking, the engineer's notes and your report. If needed, we'll talk to you or arrange to revisit the property.
  3. We give you a full written response within 14 days of receiving your complaint. It will explain what we found and what we'll do. If we need longer, we'll tell you why and when you'll hear from us.

If we got something wrong, we'll put it right. That might mean redoing a test at no cost, correcting a report, or giving a refund.

3. If you're not happy with our response

If we can't resolve your complaint, you can take it further:

  • NAPIT: we're registered with NAPIT, which can look into complaints about the technical standard of our electrical work. napit.org.uk
  • TrustMark: we're TrustMark registered (licence 2585335). TrustMark can help with complaints about its registered businesses and explain the dispute resolution options available. trustmark.org.uk
  • Citizens Advice: for free, independent advice about your consumer rights, contact the Citizens Advice consumer helpline on 0808 223 1133, or visit citizensadvice.org.uk.

Complaints about how we've handled your personal information can also go to the Information Commissioner's Office. See our privacy policy.
