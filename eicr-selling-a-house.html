Buying and selling questions

Is an EICR a legal requirement when selling a house?

Not for a home you live in. The EICR rules in England are for landlords. Buyers, surveyors and solicitors often ask about the electrics, though, so a recent report can help.

How old can the report be?

There is no fixed rule for a sale. For a home you live in, a test every 10 years is recommended, and when a new owner moves in. A buyer will usually be happier with a recent report.

Can a buyer book the EICR?

Yes. You will need the seller's agreement for the visit, usually arranged through the estate agent. Someone aged 18 or over must be there to let us in.

What is the difference between an EICR and a homebuyer's report?

A homebuyer's electrical report is a full EICR plus a written report with the repairs priced, to help you negotiate. It costs from £149 + VAT. An EICR on its own is from £79 + VAT.

Does an Unsatisfactory report stop a sale?

No. It means at least one C1 or C2 needs putting right. You can have the work done before you sell, or share the report and our quote with the buyer so it can be agreed between you.
