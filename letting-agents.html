Why agents use us

The EICR and the fixes, in one visit.

No second visit

We fix the common faults while the engineer is already there. No remedial visit to book, no chasing the landlord for a second quote.

No surprise quotes

Set a spend limit for each job: we suggest £150 + VAT. Anything over it, we send you photos and a price to approve from your phone.

Evidence you can forward

One report per property, with before and after photos of every fix and the certificate references. Forward it to the landlord as it is.

How it works

From account to certificate in four steps.

  1. 01

    Ask for an account

    Fill in the short form. We set up your agent account and email you a login link. You sign our agent terms once.

  2. 02

    Raise a job in the portal

    Choose the property type, set your spend limit and press Approve and raise job. That's your purchase order.

  3. 03

    We test and fix on the day

    The engineer does the EICR and fixes what's on our price list, within your limit, with a photo before and after each fix.

  4. 04

    Certificate, report, invoice

    You get the certificate and the photo report. The invoice goes on your account.

Fixed prices cover the items above, done during the EICR visit and within your spend limit: like-for-like replacements, standard white accessories and normal access. PAT testing and smoke alarm certificates are add-ons to an EICR only. Anything that can't be done on the visit is quoted as normal, as are rewiring, chasing walls, fault-finding, parts not on the van, any other make of RCD or MCB, a whole new recessed light fitting and any circuit that fails on test. Consumer unit replacements include the certificate and Building Control notification. Questions

What agents ask us.

Already have an account?Agent log in
What if the engineer finds something not on your list?

We send you photos and a price, and you approve it from your phone. We only do the work once you've approved it. The same goes for anything that would take the job over your spend limit.

Do I get a Satisfactory certificate on the day?

Where everything the engineer finds is on our price list and within your spend limit, yes: we fix it on the visit and the certificate is issued the same day.

If something needs a quote or a separate visit, we send you the photos and the price first. Our Qualified Supervisor decides the outcome of every EICR.

How do I match invoices to jobs?

Every job has a PO number, and you can add your own reference when you raise it.

Can my landlord get the report?

Yes. Forward the PDF, or use Send to tenant / agent in the portal to email them a secure link.

Which areas do you cover?

Essex and East London. If you're not sure about a property, put its postcode in your request or email us at bookings@testmyelectrics.com and we'll tell you.

What if the tenant isn't in?

The engineer waits 5 minutes, takes a photo of the door and leaves. The visit is charged in full, as set out in the agent terms.

How do I log in once I have an account?

Go to Agent log in, type your email address and we'll email you a login link.
