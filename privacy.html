Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version

  • We use your details to book, carry out and certify your tests, take payment and keep the records we have to keep.
  • We share them only with the people and services that help us do that, such as our engineers, our booking, email and payment providers, and your tenant or agent if you ask us to.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • Website analytics and advertising cookies are off unless you accept them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

TestMyElectrics is a trading style of UK Sustainability Group Limited (company number 13349877), Unit 21, Industrial Estate, Old Church Road, East Hanningfield, Essex CM3 8AB. We're the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For anything to do with your personal information, email bookings@testmyelectrics.com or write to us at the address above.

2. What we collect

  • When you book: your name, email address and phone number, the property address and postcode, your billing address, details about the property such as its type and number of bedrooms, the tests you've booked, and your appointment.
  • Access details: how we'll get in, and the names and contact details of a tenant or letting agent if you give them to us.
  • When you pay: payments are taken by Stripe. We receive confirmation of the payment but never your full card details.
  • When we do the work: test results, observations, photos where needed, certificates, quotes and invoices.
  • When you contact us or ask for a callback: the details you give us.
  • Your online account: we email you a login link and record when you sign in.
  • When you use our website: if you accept cookies, information about how you use the site and which adverts brought you here (see section 7).

4. Who we share it with

  • Our engineers and office team.
  • Service providers who help us run the business: our booking system host (Railway), our email delivery service (Resend), our payment provider (Stripe), and, if you accept cookies, Google (Analytics and Ads) and Microsoft (Clarity).
  • Your tenant, letting agent or managing agent, where you ask us to deal with them.
  • Our registration schemes, NAPIT and TrustMark, where they need it for audits, assessments or complaints. If we carry out work that must be notified under the Building Regulations, we register it with your local authority through NAPIT.
  • HMRC, the police or other authorities, when the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for other organisations' marketing.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers may process information outside the UK, for example in the USA. When they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or approved safeguards, such as the International Data Transfer Addendum, to protect it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Booking, job and financial records: six years after the end of the financial year they relate to.
  • Certificates and test results: for as long as they're useful for your next inspection, normally up to 10 years.
  • Callback requests that don't lead to a booking: 12 months.
  • Website analytics: up to 14 months.

7. Cookies and similar technology

Our website only uses analytics and advertising cookies (Google Analytics, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity) if you accept them in the cookie banner. Until you do, they stay off. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our website.

Microsoft Clarity shows us where visitors click and scroll, as heatmaps and recordings of visits, so we can make the site easier to use. It hides what you type into forms, and we don't use it to identify you.

We remember your choice in a small cookie called "cookie_consent", shared with our booking page so it doesn't ask you again.

If you arrive from an advert, our website remembers the advert's campaign details, such as the source and click ID, for the rest of your visit. It passes them to our booking page so we can see which adverts lead to bookings. They're kept in your browser's session storage and are cleared when you close the tab.

Our booking page has its own cookie banner for the cookies it uses.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that's wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our using it for our legitimate interests;
  • ask for information you gave us in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

Some of these rights have exceptions. For example, we may need to keep financial records even if you ask us to delete them. To use your rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We'll reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, phone 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy if the way we use personal information changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
