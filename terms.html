2. These terms

These terms apply when you book a domestic EICR, PAT testing, or a smoke, heat and carbon monoxide alarm check with us. Commercial work, testing programmes for several sites, and remedial work are covered by these terms together with the written quote we give you. If the quote says something different, the quote applies.

If you're a consumer, you have legal rights in relation to services that aren't carried out with reasonable care and skill. Nothing in these terms affects those rights.

3. Our services

  • Domestic EICR: an inspection and test of the fixed electrical installation, in line with BS 7671 (the IET Wiring Regulations) and IET Guidance Note 3. You receive an Electrical Installation Condition Report.
  • PAT testing: inspection and testing of portable appliances, in line with the IET Code of Practice for In-service Inspection and Testing of Electrical Equipment. You receive a register of the items tested.
  • Smoke, heat and CO alarm check: a test, interlink and battery check of smoke, heat and carbon monoxide alarms, with reference to BS 5839-6.

Our work is carried out by qualified electricians. We're registered with NAPIT.

5. Additional charges

Sometimes the job on the day is bigger than the booking, or there are costs we can't know in advance. These additional charges may apply:

  • Extra circuits, items or alarms above the number included in your booking, at the rates in section 4. Our engineer counts these on site.
  • Parking and congestion charges, if we have to pay them to attend your property. These are added to your final bill at cost.

We'll tell you about any additional charges and send you a final bill through your online account.

Any additional charges must be paid before your certificate is released. As soon as your final bill is paid, we'll issue your certificate or report. If there are no additional charges, your certificate is issued without any further payment.

6. Booking and payment

You can book online by choosing your tests, a date and a morning or afternoon arrival window. You pay the price shown when you book, by card. Card payments are processed securely by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card details.

Our contract with you starts when we email you a booking confirmation.

For business customers who are quoted rather than booking online, payment terms are set out in the quote.

7. Appointments, access and rescheduling

  • Please make sure we can get in at the agreed time. Someone aged 18 or over must be there, or you must arrange access, for example with your tenant, your letting agent or a key safe.
  • You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, through your online account or by email.
  • If no one is on site to let us in at the agreed time, the full charge for your booking still applies. If you need a new appointment after a missed one, it's booked and charged as a new booking.
  • The power will be switched off for periods during testing. Please save any work and switch off sensitive equipment beforehand. If anything must stay powered, such as medical equipment, tell us when you book.
  • If something outside our control stops us attending, we'll contact you as soon as possible and offer you another appointment, or a full refund if you prefer.

8. Your right to cancel (consumers)

If you're a consumer and you booked online, by email or by phone, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day we confirm your booking, without giving a reason.

If your appointment falls within those 14 days, booking it means you're asking us to carry out the work during the cancellation period. If you cancel after we've started but before we've finished, we may charge you for the work done up to that point. Once the work has been completed, you no longer have the right to cancel.

To cancel, tell us by email at bookings@testmyelectrics.com or through your online account. You can use the model form below, but you don't have to. We'll refund you within 14 days of being told you want to cancel, using the payment method you used.

Separately from your legal right to cancel, you can cancel any booking free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, and we'll refund you in full.

9. What an inspection covers

An EICR is a visual inspection and test of the parts of the installation we can safely get to. Unless we agree otherwise in writing:

  • cables hidden in trunking or conduit, under floors, in roof spaces, or inside the fabric of the building are not inspected;
  • fixed appliances and fittings are not moved or taken apart, and floor coverings and floorboards are not lifted;
  • accessories such as sockets and switches are inspected on a sample basis, in line with IET Guidance Note 3;
  • fire alarm, intruder alarm, door entry and data systems are not included unless booked separately.

These limitations are recorded on your report.

10. Results, dangers and remedial work

Your report lists any observations using these codes:

  • C1, danger present: risk of injury, and immediate action is needed.
  • C2, potentially dangerous: urgent remedial action is needed.
  • C3, improvement recommended.
  • FI, further investigation needed without delay.

Any C1, C2 or FI observation makes the report "unsatisfactory".

If we find a C1 danger, we'll explain it to you and, with your permission, make it safe, for example by isolating the affected circuit. We'll leave you a written danger notification.

We'll quote for any remedial work. It's charged separately from your test, and we only carry it out with your approval.

If you're a landlord, you remain responsible for your legal duties, such as giving tenants a copy of the report and completing remedial work within the time required.

11. Certificates and reports

We issue your certificate or report electronically through your online account, and we also email it to you. If additional charges apply, it's released once they've been paid (see section 5). You can download copies from your online account at any time.

12. Our responsibility to you

We carry out our work with reasonable care and skill. We hold £5 million public liability insurance.

Testing can reveal existing faults. Occasionally it causes worn or faulty equipment to stop working, for example an RCD that trips during a test because of a fault that was already there. We're not responsible for faults that existed before our visit, but we'll always tell you what we've found.

If you're a consumer, we're responsible for loss or damage you suffer that is a foreseeable result of our breaking these terms or failing to use reasonable care and skill. We're not responsible for loss or damage that isn't foreseeable.

If you're a business customer, our total liability to you for any booking is limited to the price paid for that work, and we're not liable for loss of profit, business or revenue.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that can't legally be limited.
